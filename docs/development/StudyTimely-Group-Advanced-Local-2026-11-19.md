# Advanced study groups — local implementation

Status: implemented locally on 19 November 2026. Not committed, pushed or deployed. Production remains V2.35.0. Payment integration and true subscription provisioning are unchanged.

## Try the feature

Run the built, noindex local fixture at http://127.0.0.1:4308/__test/group-preview and choose the host or learner. These are isolated synthetic accounts, with no real Google sign-in or external safety-review requests. The preview prepares an eight-place private room, preset tasks and one activity; no study time or attendance is invented. The entry returns to Study together. The separate production community/authentication/main-promotion gates remain pending.

## Delivered behaviour

- Free accounts: one owned active room with four places including owner. A trusted subscription or explicit advanced preview: ten owned active rooms and the applicable capacity, up to 24. Ten joined rooms belonging to others are counted separately from owned rooms. The preview uses its own account choice and server policy; it never inserts a subscription grant.
- Room list cards show ownership, members and current access. Rooms use four accessible tabs: Study together, Activities & tasks, Group progress, Manage. Activities, shared tasks and repeating activities have a secondary switch so their forms are not stacked. Membership cards use search and six-per-page pagination above six members, with one invitation placeholder at most.
- Repeating activities: weekdays, local date/time, IANA time zone, focus/break lengths, repeated-clock-time choice and next-three-date confirmation. Missing DST times are skipped. Future dates are generated idempotently from series/version/time, within an eight-week horizon. Up to five enabled series and twenty scheduled activities per room; the existing scheduled limit remains authoritative. No timer starts automatically. Editing future dates cancels older scheduled occurrences and creates new unreserved dates. Editing a single recurring occurrence withdraws reservations for explicit reconfirmation, without regenerating a duplicate. Running/past dates retain their records. Expiry pauses recurrence; renewal requires explicit review and resume.
- Shared task board: six presets, subject, optional reviewed custom title, edit/archive, voluntary participation, personal working/done/reopen/leave. Only the member changes their own participation. Group counts are shared; private personal plans and activity reflections remain separate. Completion does not create time, credits or attendance. Up to fifty active tasks per room; archived tasks/claims are cleaned after ninety days.
- Shared challenge: confirmed room minutes or activities with at least one timer start, a target and an inclusive UTC date range of up to eight weeks. One active challenge per room. Finishing a challenge does not erase time. Task completion and reservations cannot inflate progress.
- Weekly report: twelve selectable Monday–Sunday UTC weeks, daily confirmed seconds and participating-member counts, attended-activity count, daily table and real CSV download. Excludes private plans, notes and time outside the room. Retained snapshots are marked read only after advanced access ends; report snapshots are retained up to ninety days. Cache writes are throttled to one per minute per week. No attendance or time is fabricated in the UI fixture.
- Up to two owner-appointed co-hosts: activities, shared tasks/recommendations/challenges, message hiding and regular-member moderation. No role delegation, owner/co-host moderation, billing, room deletion or capacity changes. Final writes recheck current role, membership, active place and session after asynchronous content review. Roles are removed on departure/removal; advanced authority stops on expiry. Basic owner safety actions and member exit remain free.
- Room scene/music recommendations reuse the existing fourteen scenes and twenty-five licensed tracks. Each participant explicitly applies a scene or plays music. No room action autoplays audio for anyone. Pause audio stops music and ambience; volume/mute remain local. Free text is kept for activity breaks by default and may be disabled in the room recommendation settings. Sender’s own subscription/preview and existing review/moderation are independently required. Host capacity does not grant guest text chat. Production review remains disabled; custom public text fails closed when review is unavailable. Preset tasks/messages still work.
- Ending preview or subscription preserves records, keeps the selected free room and four seats active and makes extras read only. Owners can preselect the room/places in Manage. Timers stop credit at a known entitlement deadline; revoked access does not backfill an unobserved interval.

## Data and permissions

Additive migration 0014 creates the preview policy/choices, co-host roles, room atmosphere settings, shared tasks/claims, repeating-series metadata, challenges and report snapshots, plus supporting indexes. It extends activity rows and replaces ownership/join limit triggers without deleting existing rooms, time or memberships. All fourteen migrations were applied only to the isolated local database. Final index additions were also executed locally after its initial receipt. Production migration is pending. Account export includes the member’s own task authorship/participation, preview choice and co-host roles; FK cascades retain the existing account-deletion model. Group data is not imported into personal sync or browser backups.

Server-only preview policy: community_group_policy.enabled and optional ends_at. Set enabled=0 to end availability globally; existing per-account choices remain separately stored. Default choice is off for each account. On a later matched production release, decide whether this explicitly opt-in free preview should remain available before applying migration 0014; do not silently manufacture paid grants or deploy a fixture Worker.

## Changed surfaces and release constraints

Core files: server/community-entitlements.mjs, room-access.mjs, group-advanced.mjs, community.mjs, group-activities.mjs and accounts.mjs; components/Community.jsx, GroupTools.jsx, StudyCompanions.jsx, RoomMembers.jsx, RoomActivities.jsx, RoomAllowance.jsx and group-advanced.css; pure helpers src/lib/group-advanced.mjs. Updated public group guide and privacy/terms to describe actual sharing, preview and limits. No dependency, OAuth, secret, production DB/data, DNS, tracking or protected Sites-adapter changes. FocusGuide.jsx was already marked modified at task start with no content diff and was preserved.

Limits: asynchronous room-time checks cannot prove attention. Recurrence materialises dates on room reads and scheduled maintenance (bounded to twenty series per pass, rotating by touch time), not an external calendar push; there are no email/browser reminders or automatic reservation/timer starts. The current backend cap remains 24 members and 10 owned rooms. Real authenticated production acceptance, payment lifecycle and production text-review activation were not performed in this local task.
