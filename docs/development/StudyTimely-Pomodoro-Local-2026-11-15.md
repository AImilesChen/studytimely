## Delivered behavior

- Original three portrait cards, colons, English heading, phase selector, quick sound/music/alert/theme/focus controls and mode dock remain. Current full-study round and next phase are visible without expanding settings. Session settings and View routine are the new small persistent entries; an early-end/skip action appears only when relevant. Contextual actions share the auxiliary row to keep the dock in the first screen.
- Session settings and the page's header gear use a dedicated nonmodal side window; phones use a bottom sheet. Presets change study and short-break lengths only. Custom study/short/long durations, long-break interval, finite target or continuous mode, subject/new subject, independent automatic starts and reminder volume are drafts until Apply. Apply never starts/restarts a period. Cancel/X/Escape/outside discard drafts. New subjects are saved atomically with validated settings. Test sound uses draft volume without saving it; optional forced audio priming on Apply does not emit a test tone.
- Once a routine has progress, durations/target/subject are locked with an explanation and restart path. Automatic starts and alert options remain editable by the controller. Idle phase selection preserves the existing completed counter; it cannot overwrite unfinished work. New operations validate timer identity, epoch and ownership, including ready-between-period routines with completed rounds.
- End study early saves actual eligible time, excludes pause gaps, keeps prior full rounds and prepares a manual short break without adding a full round. Skip break prepares a manual study period with no learning credit. Reset current period discards its unsaved progress but keeps completed rounds/history; Restart routine resets the counter and current report while keeping saved records. Confirmations support cancellation. An operation landing at a deadline settles only its original phase instead of accidentally acting on the next one.
- Shared plan calculations distinguish planned study, the final study endpoint and a following long break. Default continuous 25/5/15/every-four is 100 study minutes, 115 minutes through Study 4 and 130 minutes including its long break. Finite four-round targets stop at 115 minutes; finite two-round targets stop at 55 minutes. No guaranteed clock finish is fabricated. Sequence details remain collapsed until requested.
- The routine report uses actual records belonging to the current root ID, distinguishes full rounds from early-ended periods and shows saved duration to seconds. Planned/current unsaved/rest/paused time are not saved totals. Task/chapter links persist across phases and records; tasks are not automatically marked done.
- Existing interrupted-study review and no-catch-up guards remain. Breaks no longer request learning declarations after long callbacks; clock-affected breaks pause at checked rest. Legacy interrupted breaks can keep checked remaining time without declaring study. Moving a running learning period pauses it for review. No dependency, storage schema, backend endpoint, authentication, credential, resource, legal or analytics-configuration changes were made. Existing four protected adapter files are unchanged.
