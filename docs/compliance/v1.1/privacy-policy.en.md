## 2. Your study data stays in your browser

Active timer state, study-session dates and durations, subject labels, goals and preferences are stored in your browser using IndexedDB. Anonymous use does not upload this database. World-clock city favourites are saved as local preferences on this device, respect Remember preferences on this device, and are not included in account sync or ordinary study backups. Signing in alone does not upload records. Choosing Sync now merges saved sessions, subjects, goals, tasks, habits, schedules and exam progress with a private cloud snapshot. The active timer and analytics choice stay on this device. If you explicitly select Include and apply appearance and sound settings during sync, those selected preferences are also included in the cloud snapshot; they do not start audio automatically. JSON import reads a file locally; export downloads a file to your device.

The built-in music player loads audio from StudyTimely only after you choose playback; it does not connect to Spotify, YouTube or the composer’s website to play music. Track choice, volume and repeat settings are presentation preferences and follow the remember/sync choices above. Playback intent and position are not stored, so music does not start automatically on a new visit. Audio delivery creates ordinary website requests as described below. Opening a composer or license link takes you to that external website.

A single active alarm and its optional reminder name, scheduled timestamp, sound settings and controlling-tab identifier are stored locally alongside the workspace. The alarm runs independently of study timers and is excluded from ordinary exports and cloud sync, including appearance/sound sync. Refreshing requires you to resume alarm checks in the chosen tab; closing the browser or device sleep can prevent reminders. Clearing local study data also clears this alarm.

Study-time calculator inputs and estimates stay in page memory and are not saved to browser storage or included in exports. Reviewing a suggested task does not save it; if you choose Save task in Study Planner, that task follows the local storage and optional manual cloud-sync choices above. Preparing a focus duration changes your timer preference only after confirmation and does not start recording study.

Other people using the same browser profile may see these records. Avoid putting your full name, school, address, health information or other sensitive details in subject labels or messages.

StudyTimely provides optional Google sign-in and manual cloud sync, with email recovery for existing unlinked accounts. It has no payments or AI study feature. Optional study groups, community nicknames and member chat are described below. The free local tools do not require an account. Optional usage statistics are off by default. Google Analytics and Ahrefs load only after you enable them in Settings → Data; no advertising or remote error-reporting SDK is enabled.

Our application does not maintain its own visitor-request database, and persistent Workers application logging is disabled. Cloudflare may process security and operational information under its own service practices; this does not give it your local study-history database.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, attachments and related delivery information to answer your enquiry or handle your request. You choose what to send. We do not need a complete study-history export or identity documents to answer a general question.

## Optional accounts and cloud sync

Google sign-in requests your OpenID identity and verified email, not access to your Google files, contacts or calendar. Existing Google-linked accounts sign in without repeating the first-registration declaration. For a new Google identity, we temporarily hold its verified email and subject in a browser-bound registration transaction usable for ten minutes. No account, cloud workspace or community access is created until you confirm the account requirements. You can cancel setup; expired transactions are removed on subsequent setup housekeeping or daily cleanup, which can add one maintenance interval. New accounts record the confirmation time and policy version, not your date of birth. Older accounts are not assigned invented historical confirmation timestamps. These records are included in account export and removed with the account. We store your stable Google subject identifier, email and account creation time. We do not use Google names or photos as public community profiles. Google processes the sign-in under its own privacy practices. Existing email accounts can use a one-time code to recover access and explicitly link the same verified Google email while signed in; matching emails alone do not merge accounts. During OAuth, a browser-bound state, nonce and PKCE transaction expire after ten minutes. If you use existing-account email recovery, the account service processes your email address to deliver a one-time code. Codes expire after ten minutes, allow at most five attempts, and are stored as keyed hashes. Rate-limit records use keyed hashes of email or network addresses with expiry. Account records contain an email, creation time and hashed session tokens. Sessions last up to 30 days unless revoked by sign-out or account deletion.

Cloudflare D1 stores private accounts and cloud snapshots in an EU-jurisdiction database. Cloudflare Email Service delivers sign-in mail. Website requests and mail may still use international infrastructure. The operator may access account information from mainland China to handle support and security requests. Optional accounts are available to users aged 16 and over; anonymous tools are intended for ages 13 and over.

Sync is an explicit choice. Snapshots support up to 1 MiB; use JSON backup for larger histories. Sync keeps deletion markers and checks cloud versions to avoid silent overwrites. In Account you can download a cloud backup, export account data, clear the cloud snapshot, sign out or delete the account and all its sessions. Local records and downloaded files remain separate. Account and cloud data remain until you delete them or we need to restrict an account for security or legal reasons. Deletion removes them from the active database. Provider recovery copies may remain for up to 30 days and are not used as active accounts. Expired codes, sessions and rate-limit records cannot be used after expiry and are removed during subsequent sign-in housekeeping. If a recovery copy is restored, applicable deletion requests must be reapplied.

## Optional study groups, chat and rankings

Room owners can schedule study activities with a subject, standard or reviewed custom title, chosen time zone, date, focus interval and optional break. Activity details, participant nicknames, selected subjects, timer-based participation, verified activity time and self-reported completion statuses are available to current room members, not anonymous visitors. Joining an activity explicitly saves a personal task on the server. Task text and optional check-out notes are available through the app only to their author’s authenticated account; they are not included in other members’ activity responses. Avoid putting sensitive information in these fields. Room themes and preset pinned notices follow the room’s existing visibility. Owners can choose a preset or submit a custom theme of 2–40 characters. New custom themes require automatic text review before sharing; the provider receives only the submitted theme through the configured review path below. Rejected drafts are not saved as room themes. The latest approved theme is retained with the room and included in the owner’s account export. Starting an activity does not automatically start other members’ timers.

Activity records, registrations, personal tasks and check-out notes are removed during daily housekeeping once the later of the scheduled date and actual start/end is more than 90 days old. A scheduled future activity is not removed merely because it was created 90 days ago. Account deletion removes that account’s participation and private notes; deleting a room owner also removes the owned rooms and their activities. Account exports include your own activity participation and activities you organise; a downloaded activity summary contains the member-visible summary and your own private task/notes, never another member’s private text. Downloaded copies remain under the recipient’s control. Private task and check-out text is checked locally for prohibited contacts and abuse but is not sent to the external content-review provider. Optional custom public activity titles require configured automatic review before sharing, using the same text-only configured review path described below. Without it, standard activity titles and preset notices remain available while free-text chat sending stays paused.

Participation is for users aged 16 and over and requires a Google-linked account plus explicit acceptance of the community rules. Anonymous visitors may browse public rooms and opted-in global rankings. Choose a nickname without your name, school, contact details or precise location. Public rooms expose their name, host nickname, member nicknames, online indicators and verified room-time totals to visitors. Private rooms require a valid invitation to join and member access to view; keep invitation codes confidential. Closing invitations or replacing the code prevents new joins but does not remove existing members. Room visibility cannot be changed from private to public.

Members can read room chat. Chat is text only, collapsed by default and hidden while your room timer runs. Members can report messages or hide another member's messages from their own chat view. Authors can delete their messages; room owners can hide messages, mute/remove members and close rooms. Authorised platform moderators can review reported excerpts and take action; reports about a room owner are withheld from that owner. Moderators are not promised to be continuously available. Contact support for an urgent privacy concern; do not share sensitive information in chat. A report retains its reason, optional details and a message excerpt for review, even if the original message or author account is deleted.

Before sharing a chat message, local server rules check for contact details, external links and certain prohibited solicitations or threats. Free-text sending also requires configured automatic safety review. When that review is enabled, we send a proposed-message detection copy to the safety reviewer identified in the chat interface: Cloudflare Workers AI (a Cloudflare-hosted Meta Llama Guard model) or Microsoft Azure AI Content Safety. The detection copy may normalise Unicode formatting to help detect obfuscation; an approved message retains its original text. Our review payload does not include your Google email, account identifier, room name, invitation code or study records. When Cloudflare is selected, Cloudflare processes the text on its Workers AI infrastructure; see [Cloudflare’s privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). When Azure is selected, processing can take place in the configured Azure resource region and associated international service infrastructure; see [Microsoft’s privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). This is a safety function for optional community chat, separate from optional website analytics. The chat interface identifies the reviewer before you send. Fixed quick study updates are resolved from a server-controlled catalogue and do not use external AI review; adding free text requires the normal review. All messages still require room permissions and abuse limits. If review is unavailable or reaches its configured capacity, free-text sending pauses; fixed updates and room timers remain available. We may reuse an approved result for the exact same text for up to five minutes, after checking local rules again. The shared review cache contains only a keyed digest, policy version and timestamps, without raw text, account or room identifiers; changes to the reviewer or our review policy invalidate reuse. Expired entries are removed on subsequent cache writes or daily cleanup, so physical retention can include one daily maintenance interval. Rejected or unavailable results are never cached. Rejected drafts are not saved in our chat or report tables or broadcast to members, although the review provider receives text submitted to it. For Cloudflare review we retain per-day aggregate usage counters and random attempt identifiers with timestamps, outcome and usage/reservation amounts for up to eight days plus the daily cleanup interval. These usage records do not contain message text, account or room identifiers. Interrupted checks can retain their conservative budget reservation. Azure review retains a monthly attempt count; both providers also use expiring abuse-limit counters. Our application does not log review payloads or provider responses. Nicknames and room names use local rules; these are not a guarantee of complete semantic review. Automated checks can miss violations or block legitimate discussion.

The room companion board shows members’ connection and timer state, optional break state and room session time. Current room members can also see recent connection information and the latest UTC date with retained verified study time in that room. This date is derived from retained room totals; no new attention tracking is performed. This is not proof that someone is paying attention. We store a per-room preference for receiving gentle interactions. Fixed quick messages can include matching self-hosted study stickers. Stickers are selected from the message ID, without per-message AI generation or review, and do not change timer state or study credit. Fixed tap, cheer and celebration events contain sender, recipient, room, type and timestamps, not arbitrary text or audio. They use no external AI review, are shown only to the intended member while fresh, and are suppressed during focus, offline periods or opt-out. Events expire after ten minutes and are removed on subsequent interaction writes or daily cleanup; physical retention can include one daily maintenance interval. Cooldowns and mutual blocking apply. Subscription-entitlement records, when provisioned, store capacity, text-sending permission and expiry; no payment integration is currently available. We also store the owner’s free-room selection and chosen free places. When an entitlement ends, extra rooms and places become read only; memberships and retained records remain, while participation and new timer credit stop. Defaults use the earliest created room and earliest joined members, including the owner. Renewing restores eligible access. These selections, preferences, own retained interactions and entitlement records are included in account export and removed with the account.

Your Google email and identifier are not published in room lists or rankings. Each room timer sends start/pause and periodic connection checks to the server. Server-checked intervals produce private account and room-time records; imported/local records do not affect community scores. Room members see room contributions even when you decline global rankings. Public global rankings are off by default. Opting in publishes your nickname and verified total from all rooms, including private rooms, for the selected UTC day/week or current streak; it does not reveal private room names. Opting out hides current global results and removes saved public-rank history; private verified records remain until expiry or account deletion. Rules describe the connection-gap, check-in and daily caps; these do not prove attention or eliminate every form of cheating.

Community profiles, rooms and memberships remain until deleted with the account or room. Leaving a room stops member access; prior contributions can remain in room totals. Removing a member frees their place and permits a later return through the normal join flow. Blocking prevents rejoining until the owner allows it; allowing rejoining does not add the person back automatically. Legacy removal actions and moderation-report removals keep their existing block behavior. Closing a room stops timers and new messages/joins, but keeps retained records for its owner to review. Author account deletion removes that account's profile, messages, memberships, scores, and owned rooms. Other participants' independent aggregate time can remain after an owned room is removed. Report excerpts may remain for moderation for up to 90 days. Chat is excluded from member views after 30 days; daily cleanup removes expired chat/credit-detail records after 30 days, reports after 90 days, and daily totals/rank snapshots after 365 days. Cleanup timing can add up to one daily maintenance interval. Ordinary cloud/local JSON backups are separate from community records; Account → Export account data includes your own community data.

D1 stores community records alongside account data in the EU-jurisdiction database. Cloudflare Durable Objects/WebSockets coordinate live room connections; connection identifiers and metadata can be processed on international edge infrastructure. The operator and authorised moderators may access relevant records, including reported text, from mainland China. Optional analytics is excluded from study-group and leaderboard pages; community text, nicknames and invitation codes are not analytics payloads.

## Optional usage statistics

You can enable Google Analytics 4 and Ahrefs Web Analytics together in Settings → Data → Allow Google Analytics and Ahrefs. The choice is off by default and stays on this browser; it is not included in cloud sync. Declining or withdrawing does not restrict the free tools. Enabling it permits the providers to receive visits to public tool pages and basic timer actions (start, pause and saved-session events with a timer-mode label), along with request/device information such as IP address, browser, language and screen dimensions. Providers may derive approximate location from network information.

We send only approved public tool paths, without URL queries, fragments or referrers. We exclude account and legal pages, email, custom subject/task/chapter labels, record contents, study durations and identifiers from our event payloads. The analytics context is removed on account/private-page navigation. Google Signals, advertising personalisation and user-provided-data collection are disabled in our tag configuration. We use the verified GA4 measurement ID directly and do not also load a Tag Manager container.

Google Analytics can use first-party `_ga` and `_ga_*` cookies. Our tag sets their expiry to 30 days without extending it on each visit. Ahrefs describes its analytics as cookieless, with daily pseudonymous counting rather than a persistent cross-day visitor ID; this does not mean no information is processed. See [Google's partner-site information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), [Ahrefs Web Analytics data practices](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/10247870-about-ahrefs-web-analytics) and [Ahrefs' privacy policy](https://ahrefs.com/legal/privacy-policy).

Turning the setting off removes the analytics context, stops further collection by this integration and expires this site's Google Analytics cookies. It does not erase measurements already received by providers. The GA4 property currently retains event-level data for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months, with user retention reset by new activity. These controls do not apply to most aggregate standard reports. Ahrefs records follow its provider retention practices. Contact us about applicable access or deletion rights. We do not promise that aggregate reports can be linked back to an individual. Providers may process analytics internationally; analytics data is not confined to the EU account database described above.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them or your browser clears or evicts them. There is no automatic history expiry. Private browsing, storage restrictions, device loss or a browser change can make data unavailable. Local storage is not a guaranteed backup.

Open Settings → Data to export or import a JSON backup, or clear local study data. Clearing stops the active timer and removes saved sessions, subjects, the daily goal and preferences across this site's tabs in the same browser profile. You may also delete individual sessions in Study Tracker. A JSON backup also includes tasks, habits, schedules and exam plans. An ordinary local backup excludes the active timer, account session and appearance preferences; a cloud snapshot may include appearance and sound settings when you explicitly select that option. CSV exports saved study sessions and linked task/chapter labels. Import merges records by ID and keeps an existing daily goal.

Clearing browser data does not delete downloaded files, device backups, support emails or provider-held technical records. We cannot remotely recover or delete records held only in your browser.

Support messages and attachments are kept while needed to handle the enquiry, follow-up, security concerns or legal claims, and then removed when no longer needed. The mailbox has no automatic expiry, so deletion is handled by the operator. You can request deletion at the contact address above, subject to applicable retention obligations. Provider-held operational and security data follows the provider's retention practices; disabling our application logs does not delete those records.

### Learning analysis and private reviews

Learning analysis uses saved personal learning intervals, task planning and completion history, dated daily and weekly targets, and your chosen report time zone. Older missing history is labelled unknown rather than reconstructed. Optional weekly and monthly reviews store a snapshot and any reflections you write in the browser workspace. They enter cloud snapshots only when you explicitly use manual account sync, subject to the existing snapshot size limit. No reflection is sent to an AI service by this feature. PDF and image exports are prepared on your device; you choose whether PDF and review-data exports include task details or reflections. Images contain totals and subject names. Exports are not published automatically. Saved reviews remain snapshots after source records change until you explicitly regenerate them. Deleting a review clears its text and report contents locally while keeping a minimal deletion marker to prevent stale sync from restoring it. Full workspace backup and data controls include these supported records. Feature-access checks send the normal session cookie when signed in; they do not upload study records or reflections.


### Advanced study plans

Optional advanced planning stores goals, available study windows, date exceptions, task links and remaining-work estimates, dated sessions, explicit feedback and a bounded undo snapshot in your browser workspace. The scheduling calculation runs locally and does not send task names or goals to an AI service. Planned time is not a learning record; only the existing saved timer intervals count. Supported JSON backups and optional manual cloud sync include these plan records, subject to the existing sync size limit. Session records can retain a planned-session identifier for linking actual time to the plan. Deletion markers prevent old synced copies from reviving removed plan items. A versioned local companion record preserves plan fields if an older tab rewrites the basic workspace, and is separated by workspace reset identifiers. Feature-access checks use the normal session cookie without uploading plan contents.
