## Resulting behavior

- Existing study planning, task-linked sessions, habits/rest days, schedules, syllabus/chapters, editable sourced starter outlines and personal outline reuse remain available.
- A local-time browser alarm supports sound testing, cancellation, five-minute snooze and ownership recovery after refresh. It follows device wall time and never creates study sessions. The page explains sleep, closed-browser and background limitations.
- The study-time calculator estimates hours from the visitor's own targets, with breaks between sessions and no break after the last session. Planned time is not added to recorded study history.
- Optional manual cloud sync can include appearance and sound preferences only after an explicit opt-in. Active timers, analytics choices and navigation history are excluded; sync never starts ambient audio automatically.
- PWA launch restores the active timer or the last public workspace. The last-page field remains compatible with legacy six-mode settings and private/legal pages are excluded from startup recovery.
- Self-hosted web-vitals 5.1.0 displays LCP/INP/CLS samples for the current page load in Settings → Data. Samples stay in tab memory and are not uploaded. The Apache licence and provenance are included.
