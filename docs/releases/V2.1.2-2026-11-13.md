# V2.1.2 — portrait Flip Clock cards

The local Flip Clock now uses three equally sized portrait rectangles for hours, minutes and seconds, with medium-weight Inter digits, quieter seams and both colon separators retained. The card height/width ratio is approximately 1.389. Cards are about ten percent larger than the initial portrait draft at normal desktop/phone sizes, while their width stays within the viewport. The date remains below the clock and is grouped closer to it. The Explore world clock text link is removed at the user's final request; the existing World Clock panel below the hero and footer link remain available.

Changes are scoped to the local-clock-screen stylesheet and removal of one hero link. Header, title/subtitle, existing theme palettes, five-tool dock, other timing tools and city pages retain their components. Seconds, date, 12/24-hour and Focus Mode settings continue working; no new settings, API, storage or account behaviour is introduced.

Validation before release: 95 existing tests, 61 local HTTP route/asset checks with robots/Sitemap/licences, in-app browser comparison against the chosen visual plus final user corrections, and six viewport checks from 320x568 to 1805x727. All visible digit bounds fit inside their cards, both colons remain visible, and the date and complete dock fit in the checked first screens. Two-group, 12-hour and Focus Mode states were exercised and settings restored. No local browser console errors were captured. Design QA passed; no new unit tests were added for a small styling-only change.
